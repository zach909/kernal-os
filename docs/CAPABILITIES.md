## What is NOT possible, and why

KOS apps are sandboxed single-user programs that exchange only text and pixels with the OS. They cannot open hardware or sensors (cameras, microphones, speakers, radios, batteries, printers, headsets, brain-signal or motion sensors), cannot play or record audio or video, cannot read the screen or other apps, and cannot reach a network or other machines: every connection is blocked by default and there is no firewall yet (README Rule 3 gap). Nothing in KOS is encrypted yet, so secure elements, payments, password vaults and encrypted sync are not built, and KOS will not fake them. There are no background services, so nothing runs, rings or notifies by itself. Anything needing other people, other machines, live data or a body-attached device is `none`, and where a local register or planner exists the note says it is only that.
