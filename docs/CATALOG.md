# Productivity suite catalogue

Generated by `python3 suite/gen.py catalog`. Do not edit by hand.

Every product you asked for is listed. **Scope** says plainly what it is:

* **full**: does the job it is named for, as a single-user local app.
* **partial**: does a real, useful part of it; the note says what is and is not there.
* **none**: not built, with the reason.

All apps are single-user and local. KOS apps cannot talk to other apps, other machines, the screen, audio devices or the network unless the owner grants access, so nothing here syncs, shares live, or reaches other services on its own.

**149 products: 51 full, 93 partial, 5 not built.**

Shared folders: an app that **owns** one creates it (and its permission); an app that **uses** one can look for it, but is only let in after you run `kos grant APP space:NAME --level read|write`.
